# My Favorite Series

## Power Book IV: Force
**Genre:** Crime / Drama / Action  
**Main Character:** Tommy Egan  
**Why I Like It:**  
I like how Tommy tries to build his own name and empire in Chicago. The story shows power, loyalty, and revenge. The action scenes are cool and the drama feels real.

---

## Peaky Blinders
**Genre:** Historical Crime Drama  
**Main Character:** Thomas Shelby  
**Why I Like It:**  
The show looks so cool with the old-fashioned style and outfits. I like how Tommy leads his family while facing danger and business problems. Every season has new twists that make it exciting.

---

## MobLand
**Genre:** Action / Crime / Thriller  
**Main Cast:** Shiloh Fernandez, Kevin Dillon, John Travolta  
**Why I Like It:**  
This movie has a dark story but it’s full of tension and emotion. It shows how people deal with crime and survival in small towns. I find it interesting because it feels realistic and intense.

---

### My Final Thoughts
All of these show how loyalty and ambition can change people. They have strong characters, smart plots, and show what it takes to survive in a tough world.
